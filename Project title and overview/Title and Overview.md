## Project Title
Smart Pet Feeder

## Project Overview

 Smart Pet Feeder is an automatic pet feeding system developed using ESP32. The system can dispense pet food automatically every 10 seconds. Users can also feed their pets manually using a push button.

The system can check the food level and alert the user when the food is low or empty. It can also detect the pet, count the number of times food is dispensed, and count how many times the pet comes to eat after the system starts.

Users can also adjust the food amount to 3 levels: Low, Medium, and High. The system displays food information and system status on an OLED screen, while LEDs show the current system status.
